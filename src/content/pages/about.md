I've spent more than 20 years building and fixing digital products that customers pay for: subscription streaming services, ad-supported media sites, sports platforms and online ordering for a fiber provider. I've led teams of up to 79 people across product management, design, engineering and QA, with budgets of $17M+.

Today I work with a small number of companies as a fractional product leader and run fixed-fee diagnostics for subscription, streaming, media and eCommerce businesses.

## Career

**Kinetic (Uniti), VP Digital Product Experience, 2023–2026.** Led product management and design for eCommerce acquisition at a consumer fiber brand serving 1.8M+ households, reporting to the CMO and CRO. Beat conversion goals by 10–20% and doubled conversion rates year over year.

**FloSports, SVP Product and Engineering, 2021–2023.** Led a 79-person product and engineering organization across web, iOS, Android, Roku and Fire TV. Revenue grew from $80M to $120M+ in one year, subscribers reached 750K+ across 26 sports, and the team recorded a +54 eNPS, the highest in company history.

**Circle Media (Opry Entertainment), SVP Product and Engineering, 2019–2021.** Joined as employee number two and built a free ad-supported TV channel, launched on The Roku Channel, Samsung TV Plus, Vizio and Peacock, plus a subscription streaming service for country music and lifestyle fans.

**Motor Trend Group, Director to SVP Product and Engineering, 2011–2018.** Built Motor Trend OnDemand from zero to 200K+ subscribers, ran web and app products with $40M+ in revenue and 60M+ monthly page views, consolidated 40+ automotive sites into seven, and cut department operating costs 40%.

**Kelley Blue Book, Director of Product Management, 2010–2011.** Led product management for kbb.com at 12M+ monthly visits.

**Yahoo!, Product Manager to Senior Product Manager, 2003–2010.** Took advertiser software from concept to launch, launched B2B advertising products in 20 countries in 14 months, and owned review systems that automated 95%+ of 200M+ search ads a month.

## How I work

I use product discovery, analytics and experiments to decide what to build, and I'm honest about what didn't work. In 2026 I added a new way of working: Claude does the build work, and I approve every specification and every release. It's how I rebuilt Olivia Olive Oil's site as a one-person team.
