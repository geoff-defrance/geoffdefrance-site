This site doesn't use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

**Analytics.** I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

**Booking.** If you book a call, Calendly collects the details you enter under its own privacy policy, and shares them with me so I can meet with you.

**Email.** If you email me, I use your message only to reply and to keep a record of our conversation.

Questions? Email [geoff@geoffdefrance.com](mailto:geoff@geoffdefrance.com).
