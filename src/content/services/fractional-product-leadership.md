## Who it's for

- CEOs and founders of growing subscription, streaming, eCommerce and fiber businesses
- PE operating partners with a portfolio company between product leaders
- Companies whose product team ships steadily while growth stays flat

## Sound familiar?

- "We have product managers and engineers shipping, but nobody is deciding what matters most."
- "Conversion and retention are flat, and every team has a different theory why."
- "Our last product leader left, and the board wants a plan before we hire the next one."
- "We need a CPO-level leader, but not full-time yet."

## What you get

- A product strategy and a 12-month roadmap tied to revenue metrics such as acquisition, conversion, retention and average revenue per user
- An operating rhythm: discovery, prioritization, quarterly planning and readouts your executives will use
- An experimentation program, with a test backlog and a way to read results honestly
- A team assessment, then hiring, coaching and role clarity for product managers and designers
- Build-vs-buy and vendor decisions for your platform, billing, CMS and analytics
- Product updates for your board or investors

## How it works

1. **Intro call (30 minutes).** We decide whether fractional is the right model or whether a diagnostic should come first.
2. **Two-week assessment.** Team and tech-stack scorecards, metrics review and stakeholder interviews, ending in a first-90-days plan. I used the same approach in my first 100 days at FloSports.
3. **Monthly retainer.** One to two days a week, with a three-month minimum and then month to month.
4. **Handoff.** When you're ready for a full-time leader, I help you hire and onboard them.

## Pricing

A monthly retainer based on days per week. Most engagements are one to two days a week.

## Proof

**FloSports, SVP Product and Engineering.** Assessed the product and engineering org with team and tech-stack scorecards in the first 100 days, then introduced product discovery practices across 11 teams. Revenue grew from $80M to $120M+ in one year, subscribers reached 750K+ across 26 sports, and traffic grew 24% with no increase in product or design headcount.

**Kinetic, VP Digital Product Experience.** Reported to the CMO and CRO, led product management and design for eCommerce acquisition, beat conversion goals by 10–20% and doubled conversion rates year over year.
