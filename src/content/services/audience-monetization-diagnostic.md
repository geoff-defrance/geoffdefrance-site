## Who it's for

Digital businesses that earn from subscribers, online sales, advertising or a mix, where:

- churn is eating new-subscriber growth, or trial-to-paid conversion is lower than it should be
- shoppers browse but don't buy, or buy once and never come back
- traffic from search is falling and nobody is sure how much of the audience you actually own
- ad revenue per visit is up but pages per session and return visits are down
- registration, newsletter or subscription offers convert poorly, so readers stay anonymous
- nobody on the team owns retention or the overall revenue mix

## What you get

Every diagnostic covers the audience and the revenue mix. The modules depend on how you make money.

### For subscription businesses

- **Funnel teardown.** I sign up as a customer on desktop and mobile and document every point where people drop off.
- **Cancel-flow and win-back review.** I cancel too, then review your save offers, exit survey and win-back emails.
- **Payment-recovery review.** Retry timing, card-updater coverage and dunning emails.
- **Pricing and packaging review.** Plans, trial length, annual offers, coupon codes and discounting.
- **Cohort analysis.** Churn and lifetime value by plan, acquisition channel and sign-up month, built from your billing export.

### For eCommerce businesses

- **Checkout teardown.** I shop and buy on desktop and mobile and document every point where people drop off, from product page to order confirmation.
- **Order value and promotions review.** Average order value, bundles, shipping thresholds, coupon codes and discount depth.
- **Repeat-purchase review.** How often customers come back, and what your email, loyalty and subscription offers do to bring them back.

### For ad-supported and hybrid media

- **Engagement and page-experience review.** Page speed, layout and ad load, measured against pages per session, time on site and return visits.
- **Audience-source review.** How much traffic depends on search, AI, social and referral partners, and how much comes back directly or through email.
- **First-party audience funnel.** Newsletter sign-up, registration and the path from anonymous reader to known reader or subscriber.
- **Revenue-mix review.** Advertising, subscriptions, memberships and commerce, and where a reader is worth more than a page view.
- **Site and brand structure.** For publishers with many sites or sections, whether the portfolio helps or splits the audience.

### Everyone gets

- **A prioritized fix list.** Each item rated for estimated impact and effort, split into quick wins and larger projects.
- **A 90-day experiment plan,** with the first tests ready to run.
- **A 60-minute readout** with your team.

**What this isn't:** ad-server setup, programmatic yield management or ad-tech vendor selection. Your ad operations team or partners keep those; this diagnostic looks at the audience, the offers and the product decisions around them.

## How it works

- **Week 1:** data access, kickoff call, and the funnel and site teardown

**What I need from you:** read-only analytics access, a billing or subscriber export if you sell subscriptions, order data if you sell online, ad revenue by section or page type if you sell advertising, and about two hours of your team's time.
