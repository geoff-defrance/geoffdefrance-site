## The challenge

Kinetic competes for new fiber customers online, and its acquisition targets depend on how many site visitors become orders.

## What I did

**Led product and design for eCommerce acquisition.** I led the product management and design teams responsible for the online buying experience and its acquisition metrics.

**Modernized the stack.** We completed a migration to a headless CMS (Contentful), new analytics, and new SEO and A/B testing tools, so the team could change content and run tests without long release cycles.

**Built a test-and-learn culture.** Discovery and delivery ran in parallel. The team ran dozens of experiments a year, with winning tests lifting results by 1.5% to 20%, and added AI tools to its workflows.

**Kept the team steady through a merger.** I led the team through the August 2025 merger of Uniti and Windstream.

## Results

- **eCommerce conversion beat goals by 10–20%**, consistently
- **Conversion rates doubled** year over year
- **A modern stack** of headless CMS, analytics, SEO and testing tools, owned by the team
- **A working experimentation practice**, not a one-off redesign

## What it shows

Conversion gains come from many small, measured changes to the buying flow, and from a stack that lets the team make them quickly.
