## The challenge

Motor Trend Group ran more than 40 separate automotive brand sites. Each needed its own upkeep. Brand general managers wanted to keep their individual sites. The CTO needed to cut the overhead of running so many.

## What I did

- **Set the criteria before the map.** We grouped sites by three tests: shared advertisers and monetization, related search topics, and fit of content, brand and audience.
- **Designed seven thematically related super-sites,** so related brands shared one audience and one platform.
- **Worked the trade-off in the open.** I met brand GMs and editors regularly, heard their concerns about losing their own sites, and agreed priorities with them and the CTO.

## Results

- **About 50% improvement in traffic and engagement** after launch, ranging from 20% to 100% by site
- **Lower maintenance cost** from running seven sites instead of 40+

## Stack

Enterprise WordPress for content management, Akamai for content delivery, and Adobe Analytics and Google Analytics for measurement.
