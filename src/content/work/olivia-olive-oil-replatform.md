**Disclosure:** Olivia Olive Oil is my wife's business. You can see the site at [oliviaevoo.com](https://oliviaevoo.com).

## The challenge

Olivia needed a new site its owner could update herself, a customer and order history that had to survive the move intact, and no development team to build it.

## How the work was done

Claude did the build work across three tools: a cloud development sandbox, browser-based inspection and a persistent project knowledge base. I ran it with four controls:

- **Specs before builds.** Nothing was built without a written specification I approved.
- **Testing on a full staging copy** of the site before anything went live.
- **Human-only deploys.** Every release went live by my hand, never automatically.
- **Read-only browser access** for the AI, so it could inspect the live site but not change it.

## What shipped

- **A new custom WordPress block theme** designed for the brand
- **Homepage modules staff edit themselves:** hero slides, the announcement bar and the monthly Oil and Vinegar Combo, with no developer needed
- **A gift-options plugin:** a gift-wrap fee and gift notes that flow through to the shipping system
- **A clean migration:** 236 customer accounts and 1,062 orders moved with zero emails sent to customers and zero misattributed orders
- **In-store pickup** at checkout
- **Measurement and search foundations:** Google Analytics 4, Search Console, and 109 recipes published with structured data
- **40+ production releases** in six to eight weeks

## What we found

About 67% of the store's order volume turned out to be bot and fraud traffic, mostly card testing. That changed how the business reads its online sales numbers.

## Results

- The owner now updates the homepage herself.
- Customer history moved intact, with no customer emails triggered by the migration.
- The business has a measurement baseline it didn't have before.

## What it shows

One experienced product leader, with AI doing the build work and clear human controls, can deliver what used to take an agency team. The controls are what make it safe to run on a live business.
